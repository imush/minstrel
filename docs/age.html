Age suitability

Minstrel Player app · rated 4+ on the App Store

Minstrel Player has no content of its own. It is a player and remote control for music and other media that you, or your household, already keep on your own media servers at home. It shows and plays only what is in that library.

What the app doesn't have

  • No store, streaming catalogue, or recommendations from us.
  • No chat, comments, profiles, or any way to contact other people.
  • No web browsing, advertising, or in-app purchases.
  • No accounts, and no personal data collected; see the privacy policy.

For parents

Whether something is suitable depends on what is in your own library, which the app can't judge: it plays whatever your media servers offer, and doesn't filter tracks marked explicit. To limit what a child can reach, limit what the media server shares, or use your device's own controls, such as Screen Time on iPhone and iPad or Family Link on Android.
